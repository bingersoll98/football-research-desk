How the desk works

Pass is first-class.

We would rather send a reason to sit than invent a lean to fill the page. If the number is a guess, we say so.

What we look at

  • Open → now, and the number versus consensus. Shop the hook.
  • Injuries that change a starter, not a depth chart.
  • Weather only when it can move a total: wind 15+, rain or snow, extreme heat or cold, roof status.
  • Short weeks, inflated favorites, and numbers that already left.

What we do not do

  • Guarantee profit or call anything a lock.
  • Force a Thursday side because it is primetime.
  • Count The Flyer in the record. It is a dart, graded on its own tab.

LEAN

A number we would still want at this hook. Dead past the hook we name.

PASS

Short week, dead number, injury fog, or a favorite we will not lay. Sitting is a result.

The record

The card is every lean sent in After Dark, the Friday Packet, and a Gameday add if it is new. Paper 1u. Pass is not a ticket.
