How the desk works

Confirm the number.

Two methods. Game picks price a side or a total against a house number. Prop picks price a player’s role against the posted line. Both publish Friday. Both grade Tuesday against the close.

The Friday card is ten game tickets ranked 1–10 and five prop tickets ranked 1–5. After Dark, briefs, and Gameday do not add tickets. The Flyer is off the record. Paper unit $25. Not betting advice. 21+.

Game picks — Best 10

A game pick is a spread or a total. We write a house number, then take the side of the market that is wrong versus that number. We do not pick who is better.

Board

  • Every NFL game. Every CFB game with a ranked team.
  • Open and now — Fanatics plus one other book. Juice on both sides. Shop the hook.
  • Official availability that changes a starter. No rumor.
  • Kickoff forecast only if the game is outdoor and wind is 15+, rain or snow, or extreme temperature.
  • Key numbers: NFL 3 and 7 first. CFB still respects 3 and 7.

House number

Start at current consensus. Add a prior only when you can name it and the market has not already eaten it.

  • Starting QB out or a clear downgrade — 3 on the spread. About 3 toward Under if the replacement is a drop.
  • Starting OT out — 1.5.
  • WR1 or lead runner out — 1.
  • Wind 15+ at kick — 3 toward Under. Steady rain or snow — 2 toward Under.

If the line already moved about the size of the prior, the news is priced. Then ask only whether the market overshot or stopped short. Two public power ranks (SP+ / FPI or equivalent) agreeing 3+ points off the market is a second opinion, not the house number. We do not run our own rating engine.

Score — A B C D

Four questions, 0–3 each. Total is the confidence score. Write the reason in 12 words.

  • A Price — house vs market after juice. Score if the gap is at least 1 on a side or 1.5 on a total. Score 2 if it is 1.5 / 2.5 or more.
  • B Move — open→now traveled with our side, or the number is stuck on the wrong side of 3 or 7.
  • C News — named injury, weather stamp, or availability the number has not finished with.
  • D Shop — we can get the hook or better than −110.

Minimum to publish: 3. Rank 1–10 by score, then by edge. Ties: NFL before CFB, sides before totals, hook before no hook.

Always ten

Ranks 1–4 should score 6+. Ranks 5–7 score 4–5. Ranks 8–10 are the next-best remaining disagreements at 3. If #10 is thin, the row says so. Never invent a favorite to decorate the list.

Size

Every game ticket is 1u. 2u only on rank 1 or 2, and only at score 7+ with the hook.

Game-pick nos

  • Do not fade “the public” from ticket percent alone.
  • Do not force a mix of favorites and dogs.
  • If the number already left, re-price. Do not chase.
  • No weather on a dome or a quiet 70° day.

Prop picks — Top 5

A prop pick is a player number. Ticket charts and handle bars do not vote. The book’s line is an assumption about role. We write our own role, then take the side of that line that is wrong.

Board

  • Posted line and juice on both sides, from Fanatics plus one other book.
  • Confirmed lineup context: who is out, who is starting, who is in a timeshare.
  • Expected game script — leading, trailing, or tight — because that changes pass/run mix.
  • Position matchup only when it is specific (this WR vs this CB room), not “they have a bad defense.”

Projection

Role × plays in this script × a simple efficiency.

  • Role — snap share, target share, or rush share. Use this week’s depth chart, not last year’s average if the room changed.
  • Plays — team pace and the pass/run split this script implies.
  • Efficiency — yards per target, yards per carry, or TD rate from recent work in a similar role. Do not use a career spike week as the mean.

Need a named reason the book’s role is wrong: a teammate is out, the script should tilt, the matchup is on this position, or the number did not move after news.

When it makes the 5

  • Projection is at least a half-unit of the stat past the line — 0.5 receptions, 5 rushing yards, 0.5 rushing attempts — or about 3 points of implied-probability edge after juice.
  • Rank 1–5 by that gap, then by how confirmed the role change is.
  • Every prop is 1u. 2u only if it is prop rank 1 and the role change is confirmed (starter out), not rumored.

What we prefer

Secondary receivers, tight ends, RB2s, and CFB volume props. Those rooms stay softer after news.

What we skip

National-TV hero quarterback yards unless the number is stale. Anytime TDs without a usage reason. The Flyer is not in this five.

Prop-pick nos

  • Do not use ticket percent or handle on a prop.
  • Do not project a backup into starter volume on a rumor.
  • Do not double-count a prop that is already a game-pick total in another form.
  • CFB props can sit longer off-market. Still need a named role reason.

After the weekend

  • Tuesday grades every published game pick and prop W / L / P.
  • We also mark whether the number beat the close. That is the process grade.
  • A losing week with plus CLV is a good process week. A winning week with minus CLV is a warning.
  • Sit list from Friday is not graded. Pass is not a ticket.
