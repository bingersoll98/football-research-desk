How the desk works

Confirm the number.

We build our own number for every game, only play when it disagrees with the market, size every play in units, and grade every pick against the closing line. Full record, losses included.

FRD picks publish Friday: ten game tickets and five prop tickets. Saturday and Sunday may add a ticket when a number moves our way. Every pick is graded Tuesday. Not betting advice. 21+.

Game picks

A game pick is a spread or a total. We price the game ourselves, then take the side of the market we think is wrong. We are not picking who is better.

What we look at

  • Every NFL game. Every CFB game with a ranked team.
  • Where the line opened, where it is now, and the price on both sides.
  • Official availability that changes a starter. No rumors.
  • Kickoff weather for outdoor games when it actually matters.
  • Key numbers, especially 3 and 7.

Prop picks

A prop pick is a player number. The posted line is an assumption about a player’s role. We form our own view of that role and only play when there is a named reason the line is off.

What we look at

  • Confirmed lineup context: who is out, who is starting, who is splitting work.
  • Expected game script, because it changes the pass/run mix.
  • Specific matchups, not “they have a bad defense.”

What we don’t do

  • Fade “the public” from ticket percentages alone.
  • Chase a number that already moved.
  • Bet on rumors.

Units

Every pick is sized in units, not dollars. Most picks are 1 unit; our strongest are larger.

After the weekend

  • Tuesday grades every published game pick and prop W / L / P.
  • We also mark whether our number beat the closing line. That is the process grade.
  • A losing week that beat the close is a good process week. A winning week that didn’t is a warning.
  • Every pick stays on the record, losses included.
