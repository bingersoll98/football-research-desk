How the desk works

Confirm the number.

Every Friday we publish ten sides or totals and five player props, ranked from most confident to least. The job is a better number than the close — not a hotter weekend.

The idea

Books are good at ranking teams. They are slower at repricing news and they shade popular sides. We write a house number for each game, then take the side of the market that is wrong versus that number. We do not pick “who is better.”

Process grade is Tuesday closing-line value. A losing week with plus CLV is a good process week. A winning week with minus CLV is a warning.

1. Board the market

Every NFL game. Every CFB game with a ranked team.

  • Open and now — Fanatics plus one other book.
  • Juice on both sides. Shop the hook.
  • Official availability that changes a starter. No rumor.
  • Kickoff forecast only if outdoor and wind is 15+, rain or snow, or extreme temperature.
  • NFL key numbers 3 and 7 first. CFB still respects 3 and 7.

2. Write the house number

Start at current consensus. Add a prior only when you can name it and the market has not already eaten it.

  • Starting QB out or a clear downgrade — 3 on the spread, ~3 toward Under if the replacement is a drop.
  • Starting OT out — 1.5.
  • WR1 or lead runner out — 1.
  • Wind 15+ at kick — 3 toward Under. Steady rain or snow — 2 toward Under.

If the line already moved about the size of the prior, the news is priced. Then ask only whether the market overshot or stopped short. Two public power ranks (SP+ / FPI or equivalent) agreeing 3+ points off the market is a second opinion, not the house number. We do not run our own rating engine yet.

3. Score the disagreement

Four questions, 0–3 each. Write the reason in 12 words. Total is the confidence score.

  • A Price — house vs market after juice. 1 pt on a side or 1.5 on a total to score. 2 if the gap is 1.5 / 2.5 or more.
  • B Move — open→now traveled with our side, or the number is stuck on the wrong side of 3 or 7.
  • C News — named injury, weather stamp, or availability the number has not finished with.
  • D Shop — we can get the hook or better than −110.

Minimum to publish: 3. Rank 1–10 by score, then by edge. Ties: NFL before CFB, sides before totals, hook before no hook.

Always ten

Ranks 1–4 should be 6+. Ranks 5–7 are 4–5. Ranks 8–10 are the next-best remaining disagreements at 3. If 10 is thin, say so on the row. Never invent a favorite to decorate the list.

Size

Every ticket is 1u. 2u only on rank 1 or 2, and only at score 7+ with the hook. Paper unit $25.

4. Top 5 props

Separate market. Ticket and handle charts do not vote.

  • Project from role — snaps, targets, rush share — times expected plays in this script, times a simple efficiency.
  • Need a named reason the book’s role is wrong: teammate out, trailing or leading script, a position matchup, or a number that did not move after news.
  • Minimum: projection at least a half-unit of the stat past the line, or about 3 points of implied-probability edge after juice.
  • Rank 1–5 by that gap. Size 1u. 2u only if rank 1 and the role change is confirmed, not rumored.
  • Prefer secondary receivers, tight ends, RB2s, and CFB volume props. Avoid national-TV hero quarterback yards unless the number is stale.

5. Sit, then grade

  • Five numbers we will not touch, one reason each. Sitting is part of the method.
  • Tuesday we grade W / L / P and whether the number beat the close.

Hard nos

  • Do not fade “the public” from ticket percent alone.
  • Do not force a mix of favorites and dogs.
  • After Dark, Gameday, and The Flyer do not add tickets.
  • No unofficial injury rumor. No weather on a dome or a quiet 70° day.
  • If the number already left, re-price. Do not chase.

Not a guarantee and not betting advice. 21+.
