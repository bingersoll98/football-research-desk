How the desk works

Confirm the number.

We price a fair line, then take the market only when it is off that line by enough to pay juice. The Friday Best 10 and Top 5 props are the only tickets. Everything else is context.

What we price

Start at weekend consensus. Write a fair number. Edge is market minus fair. A ticket needs an edge large enough to clear juice.

  • Side — ticket only if the gap is at least 1.5 points.
  • Total — ticket only if the gap is at least 2 points.
  • Favorite only if the number is still short versus fair. Dog only if it is too wide. Over only if implied scoring is light. Under only if it is heavy.
  • “They are better” is not a price. Sitting is first-class.

Injury and weather priors

These are the starting adjustments. We write them on the card when they are used. They are priors, not a black box.

  • QB out ≈ 3 points on the spread.
  • Starting OT out ≈ 1.5.
  • WR1 or lead runner out ≈ 1.
  • Wind 15+ mph at kick ≈ 3 points toward the Under.
  • Steady rain or snow ≈ 2 points toward the Under.
  • Quiet 70° and a dome stay off the card.

Two of three

A play needs two of these three, sourced. If two are unknown, it is not on the 10.

  • Open → now (size and direction of the move).
  • A valid split — see below.
  • An injury or weather item that actually moves this number.

Tickets vs handle

Tickets are how many bets. Handle is how many dollars. A split is only a model input when all of this is true:

  • Tickets and handle from a named feed, stamped with time.
  • The line has already moved toward the money side.
  • Ticket percent alone does not count. Handle with a stuck number does not count. “The public loves the favorite” does not count.
  • Prop markets do not get a split vote.

That is reverse line movement with a source, not a bar chart. Splits do not add points to fair. They only help a play clear two-of-three.

Data on every ticket

  • Consensus open and current number.
  • Fanatics plus one other book — shop the hook.
  • Official NFL / school injury report, not a rumor.
  • Forecast stamped with time when weather can move a total.
  • Fair, edge, juice, units, and a 12-word why that cites the edge.

We do not currently run a full EPA / SP+ / DVOA model. Fair is consensus plus the priors above. Tuesday we grade the 15 and, going forward, whether the number beat the close.

The published card

  • Best 10 — sides and totals only, mix of NFL and CFB.
  • Top 5 — player props. Not double-counted in the 10.
  • After Dark, the briefs, and Gameday do not add tickets. Gameday can kill a dead number. It cannot replace it.
  • The Flyer is a plus-money dart on its own tab. Off the record.

Size

Floor 1u on every published ticket. 2u only when the edge is well clear of the cutoff and two-of-three is strong. Default is 1u. Paper unit is $25.

No ticket

Fog on the quarterback. A key number we will not lay. Two inputs unknown. A number that already left. Primetime is not a reason.

The record

Home and Archive count graded Best 10 and Top 5 only. Pass is not a ticket. Flyer is not in the W–L.
