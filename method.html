How the desk works

Confirm the number.

The only tickets are the Friday Best 10 and Top 5 props. Everything else is context. Paper size is on the row.

The model

Start at consensus. Write a fair number. Lean only when the market is off that fair number by enough to pay juice.

  • Fair side: consensus ± injury points (QB out ~3, starting OT ~1.5, WR1 ~1, lead RB ~1).
  • Fair total: consensus ± weather (wind 15+ ~3 toward Under, rain/snow ~2 toward Under).
  • Side ticket only if |market − fair| is at least 1.5. Total ticket only if the gap is at least 2.
  • Two of three required: open→now move, tickets vs handle, injury or weather that moves it. Two UNKNOWN = no ticket.

How a lean is allowed

  • Favorite — the number is still short versus fair.
  • Dog — the number is too wide versus fair.
  • Over — implied scoring is light versus fair.
  • Under — implied scoring is heavy versus fair.
  • “They are better” is not a ticket. Sitting is first-class.

The card

Best 10 sides and totals. Top 5 props. That is the published record. After Dark, briefs, and Gameday do not add tickets. The Flyer is a dart on its own tab.

  • Floor 1.0u on every ticket. 2.0u only when the edge is loud and two-of-three is strong.
  • Stamp open, now, fair, juice, and a 12-word why.
  • Tuesday we grade the 15 and, going forward, whether we beat the close.

Sources: consensus plus two books, official injury report, forecast stamped when weather is used. Not betting advice. 21+.
